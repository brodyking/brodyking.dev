End of Life Notice for Pouchtrack.net and Chipledger.com.

Published Nov 17, 2026

Pouchtrack.net and Chipledger.com have come to an end. I have gained valuable experience building these websites, but I have been unable to maintain these both while working on new projects and studying computer science.

These websites built a foundation for learning basic web development, such as PHP and SQLite, but having actual users means being responsible for data, and I cannot confidently fulfil that duty while working on my studies. Thank you to all the kind people that have used these websites and provided feedback.

Chipledger and Pouchtrack remain fully open source and available on Github. Feel free to fork and continue to develop these sites.
